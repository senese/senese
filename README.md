## Hi there! 👋

I'm a Full Stack Engineer with 5+ years of experience in the tech industry! While my main expertise is backend — building integrations, crafting business logic, designing REST APIs, and handling asynchronous tasks — my not-so-secret passion for design pushed me to explore the frontend world as well.
That means I’ve also been creating SPAs, building complex forms, styling custom components, and tweaking UI/UX to make things both functional and pleasant. Being inside both worlds, I care a lot about: Security, Caching & Performance, Responsiveness and Scalability.

And of course, **always** writing clean and maintainable code.

## About me 👨🏻

### 🎓 Education

I hold a degree in Information Engineering from the Federal University of ABC (UFABC), where I developed a solid foundation in problem-solving, design patterns, algorithms and data structures. Now I'm a student of the Full Cycle School - Full Cycle Development course.

### 💻 Work

I’m currently working at TIM S.A., a major telco company in Brazil, acting as both Software Engineer and Solution Architect for a brand new product based on the Digital Twin concept. The goal is to simulate interfaces and network platforms through containers in order to perform Acceptance Test Procedures (ATP) for new platforms being integrated into TIM’s network.
